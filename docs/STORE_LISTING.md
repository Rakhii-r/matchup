```
SIXFOUR 24 is the ultimate grassroots box cricket & turf sports matchmaking platform. Whether you have a full squad looking for competitive fixtures or you are a solo player looking to join an open game near you, SIXFOUR 24 connects you to the local sports action in seconds.

⚡ KEY FEATURES

🏏 SQUAD CREATION & MATCHMAKING RADAR
• Create your box cricket team with custom logos, home grounds, and squad formats (4v4, 6v6, 8v8, 11v11).
• Radar Matchmaking: Discover and challenge matching opponent squads within your city and radius.
• Head-to-Head Challenges: Send formal match invites, accept challenges, and lock in fixtures.

🎯 POST OR JOIN OPEN MATCHES
• Missing players for your game? Post an open match and let nearby players join instantly.
• Looking for a game tonight? Browse nearby open matches, view player skill levels, and take a seat on the match sheet.

🏟️ DIRECT TURF & VENUE BOOKING
• Explore premier box cricket arenas and sports turfs across your city with real-time court availability.
• View transparent pricing, turf specifications, operating hours, high-res photos, and player ratings.
• Direct 24/7 slot booking with instant confirmation.

🪙 DIGITAL COIN TOSS & SQUAD LINEUPS
• Automated, tamper-proof server-side coin toss between captains.
• Captains pick playing lineups from their team roster before the first ball.
• Toss winner chooses to Bat or Bowl with instant notifications to both squads.

📊 LIVE BALL-BY-BALL SCORING & INTERACTIVE SCORECARDS
• Professional box cricket scoring console: record runs, boundaries, wickets, wides, no-balls, and extras.
• Live scoreticker and ball-by-ball commentary for teammates and spectators.
• Comprehensive post-match scorecards featuring team innings breakdown, batsman strike rates, bowler economy, and Player of the Match honors.

💬 CAPTAIN COORDINATION & NOTIFICATIONS
• Direct captain-to-captain match center for venue coordination and timing updates.
• Instant push notifications for incoming challenge requests, toss results, and match reminders.

---

🔒 FAIR PLAY & COMMUNITY
SIXFOUR 24 is built for genuine sports lovers. We enforce player reliability ratings, roster verification, and strict anti-harassment community guidelines.

Download SIXFOUR 24 today and take your box cricket game to the next level!
```
