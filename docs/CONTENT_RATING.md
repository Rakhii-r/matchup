# IARC Content Rating Questionnaire Answers for SIXFOUR 24

Use these answers when completing the **Content Rating** questionnaire in the Google Play Console (IARC System).

---

## 1. Category Selection
- **Category:** **Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other** (or **Sports / Social**)

---

## 2. Questionnaire Answers

| Question | Answer | Notes / Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Does the app contain violence?** | **NO** | The app contains sports matchmaking and scorekeeping only. |
| **Does the app contain sexual content or nudity?** | **NO** | No sexual themes or explicit content. |
| **Does the app contain profanity or crude humor?** | **NO** | No offensive language or crude content. |
| **Does the app contain references to drugs, alcohol, or tobacco?** | **NO** | None. |
| **Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content through voice, text, or photo sharing?** | **YES** | Allows text chat between squad captains for match coordination and avatar photo uploads. |
| **Does the app share the user's current and precise physical location with other users?** | **NO** | The app displays general distances/areas (e.g. "Madhapur · 2.4 km away") and venue locations, but does not broadcast exact real-time live GPS tracking of users to other players. |
| **Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods?** | **NO** | No in-app purchases or digital subscriptions (venue booking is direct turf booking). |
| **Is the app a web browser or search engine?** | **NO** | Dedicated native sports mobile application. |
| **Does the app contain news or educational content?** | **NO** | Dedicated to sports management and matchmaking. |
